 -  Added the `raw-property-iri-invalid` rule, which reports property IRIs
    passed to `getRawPropertyValues()` and `getRawPropertyValue()` that throw
    at runtime, such as terms and JSON-LD keywords, and compact IRIs such as
    `as:content`, which are looked up literally.  With ESLint and Oxlint, the
    `modules` option adds other vocabulary modules generated by
    `@fedify/vocab-tools` to the default `@fedify/vocab`.  [[#1272], [#1356]]
